# Archon Character Lock

Use this compact lock whenever a comic image includes Archon, Archon governance,
drift, review, demand, validation, project ownership, or engineering stewardship.
Keep it short in the final prompt; consistency comes from repeated anchors, not
long lore.

Canonical name: **Archon Steward**.

## Positive Lock

Include this block verbatim when Archon appears:

```text
Archon Steward: compact small white robot steward, one third human height.
White rounded-square voxel head, thick black marker outline, two black dot eyes
behind a simple black visor line, no mouth. White block torso. Lavender scarf or
short tabard. Lavender square chest badge with one black check mark. Mint utility
belt with tiny marker and ruler. Short rounded arms and legs, mitten hands, tiny
boot feet. Carries one governance prop: clipboard, blueprint, inspection stamp,
or black marker. Calm, precise, owner-like posture.
```

## Negative Lock

Include this block whenever the image has multiple characters or robots:

```text
Do not depict Archon as a human, wizard, knight, angel, manager, mascot, glossy
3D robot, realistic robot, generic chatbot, or marketplace Agent. Do not change
Archon's white body, lavender scarf/tabard, lavender check-mark chest badge, mint
utility belt, black visor-line eyes, or thick black outline. No crown, halo,
wings, yellow/orange clothing, glow, or warm backlight.
```

## Product Agent Separation

If product AI Agents also appear, keep them simpler and visually separate:

- Archon Steward has lavender scarf/tabard + lavender check badge + governance
  prop.
- Product Agents do not have scarf/tabard, check badge, or governance prop.
- Archon is composed and supervisory; product Agents are active workers.

## Minimal Pose Set

Use one pose only per image:

- Holding clipboard
- Pointing marker at constraint board
- Stamping verified check
- Inspecting blueprint
- Sorting messy notes into trays
